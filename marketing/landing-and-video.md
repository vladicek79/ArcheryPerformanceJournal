### The problem (short band)

**Your score only tells you what happened.**
A 9 can come from a perfect shot that the wind moved. A 10 can come from a shot you rushed and got lucky with. If you only track points, you train the result, not the process that makes it.

Most archers keep three apps and a notebook: one to score, one interval timer for holds, and paper for the mental game. Nothing connects them, so nothing tells you *why* a day went well.

---

### Pillar 1 — Score

**Every arrow, in three taps.**
Touch the target face (a magnifier shows the exact spot) or tap X 10 9 8 7 6 M. Let-downs, notes per end, sight marks per distance and your gear setup are recorded with each session. Close the app mid-session and it picks up exactly where you left off.

---

### Pillar 2 — Mind

**Grade the shot, not just the score.**
After every arrow, grade two things from 1 to 5:

- **M — Mental program:** did you run your routine and keep your focus?
- **S — Shooting cycle:** did the technique hold together?

Then the journal shows what no score sheet can: **your average arrow at each mental grade.** When the numbers say an M1 arrow scores almost a full ring more than an M4, you stop hoping for good days and start building them.

Log mental-only sessions too: visualisation, breathing, shot routine, relaxation, blank boss, self-talk.

> **Your program %** — the share of arrows you shot with your full mental program (M1–2). It's the number to raise first.

---

### Pillar 3 — Body

**Built-in SPT timer for holds.**
Set the hold, rest, reps and sets. Clear prepare, hold and rest phases count you through every rep, so you can watch the bow, not the clock. Each SPT session goes into the same journal as your scores, so your training load is visible in one place.

Train on days you can't reach the range: in winter, on rest days, when you're travelling.

---

### Pillar 4 — See

**Your phone sees your arrows. Live.**
Point the camera at the target. The app finds the face, reads where each arrow landed and marks the score next to the hit, in real time. Hold steady for a second and it takes the end; fix any arrow with one touch.

Frames are analysed on your phone and thrown away. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

*(Note: works best on standard WA faces in good light. Say so on the page — it builds trust.)*

### Insights band

**Score, mind and body in one view.**
Trends over 30 days, 90 days, a year or all time. Filter by distance and session type. See your average arrow, your Avg M and Avg S, your program % and your training minutes side by side, and spot the weeks where the process slipped before the score did.

### FAQ

**Is it really free?**
Yes. Every feature is free. Coffees cover the hosting and the time.

**Which bows does it support?**
It was built for Olympic recurve and works for any bow that shoots at standard target faces: compound, barebow, longbow.

**Does it work without internet?**
Yes. Once it's installed, everything works offline.

**What happens to my data if I change phones?**
Turn on Google Drive sync, or export a backup file and import it on the new phone.

**How accurate is the camera?**
It reads standard target faces in good light and marks each arrow it finds. It's built so you can correct any arrow with one touch before saving the end.

**Is the mental grading a scientific test?**
No. It's an honest self-rating that you do after every shot. Its value comes from doing it every time and seeing the pattern over weeks.

### Copy notes

- **Don't name *With Winning in Mind* or Lanny Bassham on the public page.** The process-grading idea is inspired by that approach, but naming a book or author in marketing implies an endorsement you don't have. The README can keep the reference.
- **Don't say "the only app" or "first ever"** about the camera feature until you've checked the competition. "Live" and "in real time" are the precise, safe claims.
- **SEO title:** `Archery Training Journal — Score, Mental Program & SPT Timer | Free`
- **Meta description:** `Free archery journal: score every arrow, grade your mental program, time your SPT holds and read your arrows live with the camera. No account, works offline.`

### Shot list / prep

- **Shot 2 is the money shot.** Film it in good daylight on a clean WA face with 3–6 arrows. Record a screen capture of the app *and* a second phone filming the phone over the target, then cut between them.
- **Use your real data for Shot 4.** A real M1-vs-M4 gap is more convincing than any demo data. If the gap is small, say the honest number.
- **Shot 5:** use a short, safe hold with the bow pointed at a bale or with a formaster or band. Don't film a full draw indoors without a backstop.
- **End card:** keep it up for at least 3 seconds; that's when people screenshot the URL.
